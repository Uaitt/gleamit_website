Gleamit is made by **Lorenzo Zabot, Milan, Italy** ("we", "us").

## In short

- Everything you record stays **on your device**. There is no Gleamit account
  and no Gleamit server, so we never see your data.
- The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- The app only talks to others for two optional things you choose yourself:
  the **Pro purchase** and the **cloud backup**.

## What the app stores

Your brushing and floss sessions, streaks, reminders, toothbrushes, dentist and
appointment details, tooth notes, the documents and photos you attach, and your
settings. All of it stays on your device. Reminders are local notifications:
there is no push server.

## Pro purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play or the App Store and checked
by **RevenueCat**. They receive the purchase details, an anonymous ID created
by the app and basic device information, to confirm the purchase and prevent
fraud. Your oral hygiene data is **never** sent.

- Google Play: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- Apple: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- RevenueCat: [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)

## Cloud backup

It is off until you turn it on. When on, Gleamit copies your records, documents
and photos into **your own** cloud storage, never ours:

- **iOS:** a hidden Gleamit folder in your iCloud Drive.
- **Android:** a hidden Gleamit folder in your Google Drive. Gleamit asks
  Google only for access to that folder (`drive.appdata`) and cannot see your
  other files. It receives your email address, to show which account is
  connected, and an access token kept on your device. Neither is sent to us or
  to anyone else.

The files are not encrypted by Gleamit. Apple and Google protect them as they
do the rest of your storage. Only the latest copy is kept. Your phone's own
system backup does not include Gleamit data: you restore from inside the app.
Backups can stop, for example when your storage is full, and the app tells you
when that happens.

## Exports

The backup archive and the dentist report PDF are created on your device. They
go only where you send them.

## Website

This website, **gleamit.app**, is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps ordinary
server logs. It has **no analytics** and sets no cookies of its own. The theme
switch saves your choice in your browser's local storage, and that choice never
leaves your device.

The home page also shows content from other sites, which see your IP address
and browser when it loads:

- the **Product Hunt** and **PeerList** badges. Product Hunt also sets
  `__cf_bm`, a 30 minute Cloudflare bot-check cookie.
- one ad from **Ad Swap**, in a sandboxed frame that cannot read this website.
  It is served from Google Firebase.

Blocking third-party content in your browser hides them.

- GitHub: [docs.github.com/site-policy](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- Product Hunt: [producthunt.com/privacy](https://www.producthunt.com/privacy)
- PeerList: [peerlist.io/privacy](https://peerlist.io/privacy)
- Ad Swap: [ad-swap.web.app/privacy.html](https://ad-swap.web.app/privacy.html)

## Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or uninstall it. A cloud backup stays in your storage
until you delete it: in your iCloud settings on iOS, or by disconnecting
Gleamit from your Google Drive on Android. The [Support page](/support/) shows
how.

RevenueCat keeps a record of your Pro purchase, even after you uninstall. To
have it deleted, email [support@gleamit.app](mailto:support@gleamit.app) with
the order number from your store receipt. This does not refund the purchase,
and tapping "Restore purchase" later creates the record again.

## Your rights

We never receive the data you record, so you manage it yourself as described
above. For the Pro purchase record we are the data controller, RevenueCat
processes it for us, and the legal basis is the purchase contract (Article
6(1)(b) GDPR). You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export it, or
object to its use, by email. You can also complain to the Italian data
protection authority, the Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali.
