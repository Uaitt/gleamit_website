Support

Gleamit keeps your data on your phone. This page explains what the app does with your optional cloud backup and how to remove it yourself.

Android

What the backup switch does

When cloud backup is on, Gleamit uploads a copy of your data to a hidden app-data area of the Google Drive account you picked. Only Gleamit can see that area. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

Turning backup off, or disconnecting the account, does not delete the copy already in Drive. It only stops new uploads. The copy stays in your Drive until you delete it there.

Delete the backup from Google Drive

  1. Open the Google Drive app, or go to drive.google.com in a browser.
  2. Tap the menu (three lines) or your profile picture, then Settings.
  3. Choose Manage apps. On the web this is under Settings → Manage apps.
  4. Find Gleamit in the list.
  5. Tap Options next to it, then Delete hidden app data. Confirm.
  6. Optional: choose Disconnect from Drive to revoke Gleamit's access entirely.

If you connected more than one Google account over time, repeat these steps in each account.

iOS

What the backup switch does

When cloud backup is on, Gleamit writes a copy of your data into its own container in your iCloud Drive. The container is hidden from the Files app so it cannot be moved or deleted by accident. iCloud does the uploading, following your iCloud settings.

Turning backup off does not delete the copy already in iCloud. It only stops new uploads. The copy stays in your iCloud storage until you delete it there.

Delete the backup from iCloud

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then iCloud.
  3. Tap Manage Account Storage (on older iOS versions: Manage Storage).
  4. Find Gleamit in the list of apps using iCloud storage.
  5. Tap it, then Delete Data from iCloud. Confirm.

On a Mac: System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage → Gleamit → Delete Documents and Data.

Delete all Gleamit data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone: sessions, teeth, photos, documents, reminders and settings. If cloud backup was ever on, also follow the platform steps above so no copy remains in your cloud.

Your Pro purchase is tied to your App Store or Google Play account, not to the app data. Reinstall and use Restore purchases to get it back.
