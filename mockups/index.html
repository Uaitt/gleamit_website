Common questions

Questions people ask

Where is my data?

On your phone, inside the app's own storage. Gleamit has no servers and no accounts, so there is nowhere else for it to be.

Can you see my data?

No. There is nothing on our side to look at. The only network calls are the store's purchase check for Pro and, if you switch it on, the backup to your own iCloud or Google Drive.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Restore it on a new phone with the same store account.

How do I move to a new phone?

Use Gleamit's full export to make a zip, then import it on the new phone. This works across Android and iOS too. If cloud backup was on, restore from it on first launch.

Is the backup encrypted?

Your backup is ordinary files in a private area of your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Apple or Google protect them the same way they protect the rest of your cloud storage. Gleamit does not add its own encryption.

Does Gleamit give medical advice?

No. It records what you enter and shows it back to you. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or grade anything. Your dentist does that.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app and all data goes with it. If you used cloud backup, remove the backup from your iCloud or Google Drive as well. The support page has the exact steps.
